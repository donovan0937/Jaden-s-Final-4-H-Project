readMe.txt

Hello state judges, my name is Jaden Davisson, and I am pleased to have you see my final 4-H project. It is a custom html website, built from the ground up, with the goal being a digital scrap book of past projects I made during high school.


FAQ:

- Q: What inspired you to make this project?

- A: I really enjoy front end programming, and have been competing in the website category on and off over the years. I was inspired to make this specific website by seeing all sorts of old internet blogs made by people in the early 2000s and late 90s, as well as the ARG pages of deltarune.com.

- Q: What programming languages did you use in this project?

- A: The website contains HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript.

- Q: What challenges did you face when making your project?

- A: I encountered many bugs when attempting to make certain elements of the webpages, for example I wanted to reference the background of repeating images that can be found on a lot of older websites, but I just couldn’t figure it out on my own.

- Q: How did you overcome those challenges?

- A: I looked for internet guides and tips on how to do certain things and figured out how to make the specific elements I wanted. A great website that I spent hours just reading was www.w3schools.com, I learned so much stuff there that I couldn’t even use it all!

- Q: What was your favorite part of this project?

- A: Expanding my programming knowledge beyond what I learned in school, and playing around with different functions.
